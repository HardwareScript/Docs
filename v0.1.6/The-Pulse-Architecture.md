## 3. Scaling the Pulse (The Multi-Domain Mapping)

The Pulse Architecture is "Domain-Agnostic." It treats the following three pillars as simple variations of the same "Pulse-State" logic:

### A. The Electrical Pulse (Conductivity)
*   **Mapping:** Resistance is modeled as **Inertia**.
*   **Logic:** A high-resistance material (like Polysilicon) requires more Ticks to move energy from Voxel A to Voxel B than a low-resistance material (like Aluminum).
*   **Simulation:** "How many Ticks until the output Voxel matches the input Pulse?"

### B. The Photonic Pulse (Light)
*   **Mapping:** Refractive Index is modeled as **Phase Delay**.
*   **Logic:** Light moves through a vacuum at "Max Velocity" (1 Voxel per Tick). In Glass, it is slowed down (1 Voxel per 1.5 Ticks).
*   **Simulation:** The engine calculates the interference pattern based on the **Phase-Offset** of the pulses.

### C. The Thermal Pulse (Heat)
*   **Mapping:** Thermal Conductivity is modeled as **Diffusion Latency**.
*   **Logic:** Heat is a slow pulse. It might take $1,000,000$ Ticks to move across the same space that an electron crosses in $1$ Tick.
*   **Simulation:** The engine tracks the "Energy Density" of each Voxel and propagates it to neighbors at a rate defined by the material's **Thermal Tick Rate**.
